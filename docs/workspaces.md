# Workspace

The **workspace** is the configuration that contains all the relevant information about your Hopkey setup (sessions,
integrations, app preferences, etc.).

It's private and belongs to a **single machine**: it contains your personal preferences and **all the sessions and
integrations that you created yourself**. If you need to migrate your configuration to another machine, you will have
to do it manually.

!!! Info
    Hopkey saves the workspace on your machine as an encrypted file inside your <home>/.hopkey directory, and the
    secrets of your sessions and integrations in the [System Vault](security/system-vault.md).
