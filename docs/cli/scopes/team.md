`hopkey team`
============

Login to your Team account

* [`hopkey team login`](#hopkey-team-login)
* [`hopkey team logout`](#hopkey-team-logout)
* [`hopkey team status`](#hopkey-team-status)

## `hopkey team login`

Login to your Team account

```console
USAGE
  $ hopkey team login

DESCRIPTION
  Login to your Team account

EXAMPLES
  $hopkey team login
```

## `hopkey team logout`

Logout from your Team account

```console
USAGE
  $ hopkey team logout

DESCRIPTION
  Logout from your Team account

EXAMPLES
  $hopkey team logout
```

## `hopkey team status`

Get the team login status

```console
USAGE
  $ hopkey team status

DESCRIPTION
  Get the team login status

EXAMPLES
  $hopkey team status
```
